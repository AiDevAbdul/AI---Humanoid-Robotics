# Control Systems

Control systems are essential for robotic applications, enabling precise movement and task execution. This chapter covers the fundamentals of robotic control.

## What are Control Systems?

Control systems manage and regulate the behavior of dynamic systems. In robotics, they ensure that robots move accurately and perform tasks as intended.

## Types of Control Systems

- **Open-Loop Control**: Control without feedback
- **Closed-Loop Control**: Control with feedback
- **PID Control**: Proportional-Integral-Derivative control
- **Adaptive Control**: Control that adjusts to changing conditions

## Applications

Control systems are used in various robotic applications:

1. **Position Control**: Precise positioning of robotic arms
2. **Velocity Control**: Controlling the speed of movement
3. **Force Control**: Controlling the force applied by robots
4. **Trajectory Control**: Following specific paths or motions
